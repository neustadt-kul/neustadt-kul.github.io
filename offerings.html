    401 W Division St, Chicago, IL. See the map on Visit.

    How do I reach support?

    Use the Contact form or email support@neustadtkul.com. We reply during open hours.

    How do I book?

    Send a message on Contact, email bookings@neustadtkul.com, or call +1 (312) 555-0177.

    Are prices current?

    Listed prices are current and may shift with seasons — we update this site when they do.

    Do you take groups?

    Yes for many bookings — tell us the size and date and we'll confirm what Neustadt Kul can host.

    What should I bring?

    Just yourself (and any notes on allergies, sizes, or preferences). We'll handle the rest.

    Do you update services often?

    Yes — seasonal shifts are posted here first.

    Can I book for someone else?

    Yes — include their name and preferences in the Contact form or email support@neustadtkul.com.

    Is Neustadt Kul good for first visits?

    Absolutely — tell us it's your first time and we'll recommend a starting point.
